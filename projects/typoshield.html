TypoShield

Firefox extension that warns you when a site imitates a trusted brand. Typosquat and phishing protection, focused on Southeast Asia.

Anti-phishing Typosquat Scam detection Privacy

01 What it does

TypoShield checks every site you visit, entirely on your device. If a site imitates a trusted brand, you get warned before you type anything into it.

Phishing sites are blocked with a warning page that names the exact reason and links to the real official site.

Suspicious sites are not blocked. You get a badge with the risk score, a desktop notification, and a slim banner at the top of the page. Click the toolbar icon for the full verdict.

Links on every page are scanned too: phishing links get a red underline, suspicious ones yellow. Links are never blocked from being clicked — the warning is yours to judge.

It also survives redirects. A typosquat domain that bounces you to a scam survey keeps its warning on the final page.

02 How it detects

  • Homoglyphs — lookalike characters are corrected first: 0 to o, 1 to l, rn to m, vv to w. Punycode domains are decoded and Cyrillic lookalikes mapped back to Latin.
  • Edit distance — 1 character away from an official domain is blocked; 2 characters away is a warning.
  • Brand in domain — a brand name in a subdomain or label that is not the official domain, such as maybank.my.com.
  • Shady hosting — IP addresses, free hosting services, URL shorteners, and hyphen-stuffed domains.
  • Scam text — page text is scanned for prize claims, account threats, crypto doublers, and free Robux lures.
  • Scores — phishing at 50 and above, suspicious at 20 to 49, safe below 20.

35 brands are protected, including Roblox, Maybank, CIMB, PayPal, Discord, TikTok, Shopee, and Gmail.

03 Privacy

Everything is checked on your device. No browsing data, no URLs, and no reports ever leave the browser.

No account, no backend, no tracking. The brand list ships with the extension.
