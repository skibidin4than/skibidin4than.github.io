01 What it does

Starlight Diary is a private diary and chat app for a small circle of friends. Version 2 was redesigned with feedback from 40+ testers, and 60+ people use it now. There are 40 themes, and study rooms fit up to 6 people.

I have since left the Starlight dev team to focus on cybersecurity, and handed the codebase and backend over to the rest of the team. I still run occasional security checks on it and report what I find.

02 What is inside

  • Diary, create, edit, and delete entries, with exact and fuzzy search. Export your own data, or delete your account.
  • Blackholes, every diary entry becomes a star that slowly orbits a black hole. It drifts closer each day, fades toward white, and disappears on day 4, so your sky shows what you wrote lately.
  • Together, write diaries and draw together in real time, on a shared board with collaborative drawing, plus a Photowall you can set to private or public. Friends and public profiles.
  • Messages, direct messages with reactions, edit, delete, and read status. Private group chat with pinned messages, plus a global chat. Hop into a public voice lounge with lots of people.
  • Calls, voice and video calling over WebRTC, with presence (online and last seen) and typing indicators.
  • Study, study rooms for up to 6 people, a focus mode the AI can help you set up, study groups with grades and plans, and a Study Coach for understanding subjects and planning sessions.
  • Play, mini-games: Ludo, Find the Star, and Chess in 1v1, 1v5, and 1v10. Beat the 1v10 for a title and an achievement.
  • Themes, 40 complete visual skins. One theme changes the whole app: new diary papers, stars, and achievements.
  • Tracking, a mood tracker and a habit tracker with streaks, plus notifications and global search across diaries, messages, groups, and users.
  • Moderation, block and mute users, report users, and an admin dashboard with ban and unban and a moderation log.
  • Accounts, Discord account linking with PIN verification, 2FA with recovery codes, multi-device session management, and email verification.
  • AI, a personal AI that remembers meaningful context, and a Diary AI that turns entries into reflections. You decide what the AI is allowed to see, and the AI keeps working when a provider has issues.

03 Security

  • Passwords, Argon2id hashing.
  • Encryption, AES-256-GCM on diaries, messages, and 2FA secrets, with per-record binding. Encrypted content stays searchable through blind-index search, with no plaintext stored.
  • Sessions, JWT with token revocation, so a password change or a ban logs the user out instantly. Algorithm-pinned JWT blocks alg:none attacks.
  • Passkeys, 2FA and passkeys (WebAuthn), plus safer data export and account deletion.
  • Hardening, rate limiting on sensitive endpoints, input escaping against XSS, anti NoSQL injection, anti prototype pollution on socket payloads, CSRF protection, secure password reset with no email enumeration, admin checks re-validated against the database, and HTTPS enforcement with HSTS.

Security should be something users can understand and control, not something hidden behind a vague "secure" badge.

04 Shipped

Everything from the old roadmap is done: push notifications, image and media upload, diary PDF and image export, habit reminders, mood analytics and charts, a moderator role, CAPTCHA on registration, custom paper themes and fonts, an in-diary drawing canvas, voice recording and voice-to-text notes, custom tags and categories, stickers and washi tape, a master PIN with auto-lock, backup and restore, multi-language support (CN, KR, and EN), Circle Hub, and achievements (Starlight Sky). There are no more updates planned.
